Cybersecurity Awareness Month continues with a look at DCShadow, an attack that turns Active Directory's own replication against itself to push malicious changes that blend in as routine. This is part two of a three-part series on the most destructive identity attacks, and how identity and security teams can mitigate their impact. Part one of this series covered Golden Ticket attacks , which exploit the trust Kerberos depends on.
