Cohesity FortKnox , our cyber vaulting solution, helps organizations maintain a trusted recovery copy even if production environments and backup infrastructure have been compromised. FortKnox delivers the essential controls organizations need for effective cyber vaulting: An isolated, immutable copy that stays out of reach of attackers. Quorum-based approvals that prevent any single user from taking critical recovery actions.
