Today we’re bringing two new major enhancements to how organizations use Cohesity Identity Resilience: Active Directory Forest Recovery (ADFR) powered by Semperis is now built directly into the self-managed deployment of Helios, and every recovery is now automated end to end. These enhancements accelerate the “last mile” of AD recovery. Your identity, security, and backup teams can now move with greater efficiency and efficacy for identity resilience.
